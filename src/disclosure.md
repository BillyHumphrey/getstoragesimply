Get Storage Simply plans to participate in affiliate programs. If you follow a link marked as an affiliate link and later rent a unit or buy a related service, we may receive a commission. That commission comes from the company or its affiliate network, not from an extra charge added on top of your rent by us.

As of November 18, 2026, **no affiliate links on this site are live.** The buttons and slots you may see are clearly labeled placeholders. They do not use tracking URLs, and clicking them will not send you through an affiliate network.

## Planned programs

When programs are approved, we expect to use links for:

- **SpareFoot**, through the FlexOffers network
- **Neighbor**
- **U-Haul**

Each live affiliate link will be identifiable as such. In the site’s code, placeholder slots are marked with `AFFILIATE_PLACEHOLDER` comments and `data-affiliate-status="placeholder"` attributes so they cannot be mistaken for working tracking links.

## Independence

Affiliate relationships do not buy a ranking, a recommendation, or a silence. We will still report an operator’s published rate-increase policy, and we will still say when we cannot verify a claim. If a page is a paid placement or a sponsored review — we have none today — it will be labeled as such.

This disclosure is intended to comply with the [FTC’s guidance on endorsements and testimonials](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking), including the expectation that material connections be clearly and conspicuously disclosed.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a link is an affiliate link, treat any “Compare,” “View storage,” or partner button as potentially compensated once those programs are live, and read the label next to it. Until then, they are inactive placeholders.
