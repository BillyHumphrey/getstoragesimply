Effective date: November 18, 2026.

Get Storage Simply is a static website. In this starter version we do not offer accounts, newsletters, or comments, and we do not ask you to submit a form.

## Information we collect now

The computer that hosts this site — for example GitHub Pages, once the site is public — may automatically receive standard server logs such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. We do not control every log a host keeps. We do not use those logs to identify you, and we do not sell personal information.

This starter version does not include a third-party analytics script.

## Affiliate links and cookies later

When SpareFoot, Neighbor, or U-Haul affiliate links go live, clicking one may take you to a partner site or affiliate network that sets its own cookies and collects its own data under that company’s privacy policy. We will update this page when those links are turned on.

## Cookies

This static version of the site does not set first-party analytics cookies. Your browser may still store ordinary settings of its own.

## Children’s privacy

This site is written for adults making a storage decision. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.

## Contact

A contact method will be added here when the site has a public home. Until then, this policy describes the starter site as published in this repository.
