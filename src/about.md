Get Storage Simply is a simple public notebook about self-storage around Atlanta and Gwinnett County. It is compiled by Billy Humphrey, a pastor who lives in the Atlanta area.

The idea is not to out-shout the big listing sites. It is to write down, carefully, the things renters often only learn after they have already moved in: whether an advertised rate is introductory, whether a company publishes a rate lock, what “climate-controlled” means at that facility, and whether a location actually offers space for a car, boat, or RV.

## How a page gets made

Each guide starts with public sources — the facility’s own website, the operator’s published policies, and, where it helps, official climate data. We name the source and the date we checked it.

If a number or policy is not on a public page, we do not invent it. “We could not verify this” is a normal and useful sentence here.

We also do not treat a generic marketing block as a fact about a specific address. If a brand’s size-guide text mentions covered parking on every location page, but the feature list for that address does not, we say so.

## What we will not do

We will not dress this up as a guarantee of savings. Plenty of comparison sites earn little or nothing, and advertised storage rates change constantly. Our job is to leave you better prepared to ask a manager a precise question, not to tell you that a unit is “cheap.”

We will not hide affiliate relationships. Planned partners are SpareFoot (through FlexOffers), Neighbor, and U-Haul. Those links are placeholders until the programs are approved. See the [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/).
